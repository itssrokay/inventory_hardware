# ADR-001: Keep the shop catalog local on Android

- Date: 2026-11-06
- Status: Accepted

## Context

The initial shop needs fast price lookup for roughly 500–1,000 items on one Android phone, including sizes and Hindi aliases.

## Decision

Use Kotlin/Compose with Room, Unicode FTS4, aliases and typo matching. Keep prices as integer paise on variants and use versioned ZIP backups.

**Alternatives considered**

- Hosted multi-phone synchronization: deferred until the single-phone app proves useful in the shop.
- Embeddings or a vector database: deferred until search misses demonstrate a need.

**Consequences**

- Local lookup works without an internet connection, but the owner must back up the phone and other devices do not sync.
