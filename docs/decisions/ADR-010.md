# ADR-010: Keep product albums shelf photos and AI input batches separate

- Date: 2026-11-08
- Status: Accepted

## Context

The owner requested multiple uploads, shelf images and multi-image AI entry. Existing single-photo catalogs and backups must remain readable, and unrelated list/shelf images must not silently become product pictures.

## Decision

Keep the existing cover field, add ordered extra-product and shelf-photo lists in additive Room schema 3 columns, and export both in version 3 Android/web backups. Restore versions 1–3. Limit each album/batch to six images; AI accepts at most 2 MB each and 8 MB combined. Category suggestions use product images only; shelf images are excluded. Multi-image batches are read together in the Responses content array and still require owner review.

**Alternatives considered**

- One mixed image list for products shelves and AI: rejected because cover choice and what gets transmitted become unclear.
- Automatically attach AI input images to every parsed product: rejected because pages, invoices and shelf shots can describe multiple unrelated items. An explicit reuse action avoids uploading the same images again.

**Consequences**

- Old cover photos, prices and source links migrate without data conversion. Backups grow with photos and must be restored in an app supporting version 3. Product and shelf albums can be cropped, removed and viewed independently; no stock or price is inferred from appearance.
