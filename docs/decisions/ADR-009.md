# ADR-009: Separate price viewing from saved price changes

- Date: 2026-11-06
- Status: Accepted

## Context

The owner needs to check a price and calculate a discount at the counter without entering edit mode, and wants related items grouped for browsing.

## Decision

Open a product detail view from catalog cards/prices. Compute discounts from the unchanged saved price in integer paise; offer explicit Edit price and Edit item actions. Suggest up to six products from the same category with matching brands first, retaining independent product records.

**Alternatives considered**

- Use the price editor for every lookup: rejected because a temporary quote could be mistaken for a saved catalog change.
- Automatically merge related products into one item: rejected because similarity does not prove identical fittings, specifications, selling units or source links.

**Consequences**

- Counter browsing and quotes do not create price history or modify product records. Changing a price takes one explicit edit action. Related suggestions are local category/brand links rather than owner-defined collections.
